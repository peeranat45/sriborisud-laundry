01

Colour

Built from a four-colour Color Hunt palette — #8B9A6E muted sage, #F7F2EB warm cream, #EAE2D6 beige, #EEEEEE grey. Muted sage over warm cream reads as calm and physical rather than as software, which suits a 70-year-old Rattanakosin shop better than a saturated green would. Two adjustments were forced by contrast and are explained below.

What changed from the source palette, and why
#8B9A6E is not the primary action colour. Its relative luminance is 0.297, so white text on it reaches only 3.02:1 against the 4.5:1 required. It sits at sage-500 and does real work as the focus-adjacent brand tone and as a brand band with dark text over it (5.55:1). Buttons and links use sage-700 #54633B, the same hue darkened.
#EEEEEE is not used. It is a neutral grey with zero warmth in a palette whose other three colours all sit around hue 36. Given white for card surfaces and cream for the page, it has no job left — and placing it next to the creams makes both look slightly dirty. Dropping it is the right call. Say the word and I will give it the disabled-track role.
#F7F2EB and #EAE2D6 are used exactly as given — the page background and the sunken/pill background. They are the two colours covering the most pixels, so the palette's character is intact.

06

Rules

Six rules. If a screen breaks one of them, the screen is wrong, not the rule.

1 · Stage is never typed. A stage pill always shows the latest scan. There is no editable status field anywhere in the system.
2 · Colour is never the only signal. Every stage pill carries a dot and a word. Every error carries an icon and text.
3 · The scan app gets 56px, everything else 44px. No exceptions in the scan app — it is used standing, with damp hands.
4 · One primary action per screen. If two actions feel equally primary, the screen is doing two jobs and should be split.
5 · Numbers people read aloud use mono tabular. Bill numbers, prices, counts, piece totals.
6 · Corrections append, never overwrite. Any UI that changes a record shows that the original stays visible.
07

Accessibility · measured, not assumed

Every colour pair was calculated against WCAG 2.1 AA rather than eyeballed. Starting from the Color Hunt palette, three pairs failed on the first pass and the tokens were changed: muted text was darkened to #6B5E4A so it clears 4.5:1 on the beige sunken background as well as on cream, the input border was strengthened to #9A8A69, and the focus ring moved from the palette sage to sage-600 because #8B9A6E reaches only 2.71:1 against the cream page background.

What is still untested
Named here so it is not mistaken for done.
• Readability in direct sunlight at the shop counter — must be checked on a real phone at the shop, not on a desk monitor.
• Whether staff can hit a 56px target with damp hands.
• Whether IBM Plex Sans Thai renders correctly on the oldest phone any staff member actually owns.
• Whether the six stage colours stay distinguishable for someone with deuteranopia — the dot and label cover this, but worth one check.
